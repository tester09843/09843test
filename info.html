you have 6 attempts to guess an enemy correctly, if you go over 6 guesses you lose. If a section is green it means it's correct, if a section is yellow it means you are close, if it's red you're wrong and its not that close.

  • Note: to get a description of the modifiers simply click the modifier thats active when in a run.
  • Classic: the standard mode. Modifiers start appearing from wave 5 onward.
  • Hell: theres only 11 waves, modifiers start appearing from wave 1 onward, wave 11 aka wave ultima has all modifiers vitaraged at the same time.
  • Nightmare: plays like Classic, but every Sandbox exclusive modifier (including the positive ones) is mixed into the pool, and theres a lot more enemies.
  • Sandbox: pick any modifiers (and their Vitaraged versions) to keep active every wave, optionally randomize which of your selected modifiers show up using Classic's wave formula, and choose exactly which enemies are allowed to appear.
  • Guess the Item (Classic): same rules as Classic, but you're guessing items instead of enemies. Modifiers start appearing from wave 5 onward.
  • Guess the Item (Hell): same rules as Hell, but for items. 11 waves, modifiers from wave 1 onward, wave 11 (Ultima) has every modifier vitaraged.
  • Guess the Item (Nightmare): plays like Guess the Item Classic, but every Sandbox exclusive modifier (including the positive ones) is mixed into the pool.
  • Health: within 50 hp of the target.
  • Total Waves: within 6 total waves of the target.
  • First Encounter: within 2 waves of the target.
  • Quick Note: total waves count includes attrition and hell, as while no enemies debut in attrition and only 1 enemy debuts on hell the rest do appear in them, w1-9 of mastermind wont be included, as those waves are controlled by the player, enemies that debut in sandbox have total waves as 0 as sandbox isnt a wave.
  • Guess the Item — Damage/Health: within 15 of the target.
  • Guess the Item — Firerate/Cooldown: within 50 of the target.
