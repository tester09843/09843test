you have 6 attempts to guess an enemy correctly, if you go over 6 guesses you lose. If a section is green it means it's correct, if a section is yellow it means you are close, if it's red you're wrong and its not that close.

  • Health: within 50 hp of the target.
  • Total Waves: within 6 total waves of the target.
  • First Encounter: within 2 waves of the target.
  • Quick Note: total waves count includes attrition and hell, as while no enemies debut in attrition and only 1 enemy debuts on hell the rest do appear in them, w1-9 of mastermind wont be included, as those waves are controlled by the player, enemies that debut in sandbox have total waves as 0 as sandbox isnt a wave.
